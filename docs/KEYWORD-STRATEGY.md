## 7. Keyword density and placement discipline

Enforced rather than intended. `_build/check_site.py` fails the build on title width and metadata
problems; density is a review discipline.

- Primary keyword: **front-loaded** in `<title>` and `<h1>` (both follow the
  `Primary [Month Year]: Secondary` formula), then in the first 100 words, one `<h2>`, and the meta
  description. Roughly **0.4–0.8%** of body text. Never more.
- Secondary keyword: in the title and H1 after the date block, and as a natural phrase in body copy.
  One title therefore covers two queries — e.g. `/online-pokies/` targets both *online pokies NZ* and
  *real money pokie sites*.
- Secondary keywords: in H2/H3 where they read naturally, and in the FAQ questions, which is where
  long-tail phrasing belongs.
- Entities do the heavy lifting. A page that names the DIA, the Act, Rabidi N.V., Evolution, POLi,
  Neosurf and the Gambling Helpline demonstrates topical coverage far more convincingly than one that
  repeats "best online casino NZ" fourteen times.
- No keyword in an anchor more than once per source page.
- No heading exists purely to hold a keyword. Every H2 answers something.

### Rules the build enforces

1. **Tier 1 is mandatory.** A missing Tier 1 term fails the build.
2. **Anti-stuffing.** No Tier 1 or Tier 2 phrase may exceed **1.1% of body words**. The highest actual
   figure on the site is 0.32% (`no deposit bonus nz`, 11 occurrences in 3,461 words).
3. **High-volume terms repeat.** Each page's head term appears 2–11 times, in the title, the H1, at
   least one H2 and the body — never once, never to the point of reading as filler.
4. **No cannibalisation.** `/online-casinos/bonuses/` may not target `no deposit bonus nz`,
   `free spins no deposit nz`, `no deposit bonus codes nz` or `no deposit casino nz` — those belong to
   `/no-deposit-casinos/` alone. No page but the homepage may use `best online casino sites nz` in a
   heading.
5. **Proximity matching.** Several supplied terms are keyword-tool fragments rather than English
   (`rtp meaning casino`, `casino no deposit bonus new zealand`). The checker accepts an exact phrase
   *or* all of a term's words inside a short window, which is how an engine resolves them. Forcing the
   literal string would produce sentences that read as stuffing to a person and gain nothing.

The **homepage** keeps `best online casino sites NZ`. **`/online-casinos/`** owns
`online casinos nz`, `online casino new zealand`, `real money casino nz` and `top online casinos nz`.
The two do not compete, and rule 4 above stops that drifting.
